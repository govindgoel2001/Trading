# AI × Quant Lab

Four practical AI-assisted quantitative finance builds, organized as dedicated branches in this repository.

## Projects

| Branch | Build | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `prediction-market-arbitrage` | Prediction Market Arbitrage | Scans same-event pricing across prediction markets, models costs/slippage, and sizes only net-positive opportunities. |
| `information-diffusion` | Information Diffusion Model | Turns timestamped news + price moves into an event-response model using Hawkes-style intensity ideas. |
| `ai-strategy-search` | AI Strategy Search Lab | Generates strategy variants, backtests them across regimes, and compares robustness rather than just peak Sharpe. |
| `backtest-reality-check` | Backtest Reality Check | Applies multiple-testing / Deflated Sharpe style checks so a lucky backtest does not masquerade as edge. |

## Principles

- AI proposes, structures and accelerates research; it does not make a strategy valid by itself.
- Backtests include fees, spread, slippage and latency assumptions where relevant.
- Validation focuses on out-of-sample robustness and multiple-testing risk.
- Upstream open-source projects keep their original licenses and attribution.

## Educational use

These projects are for research and education, not financial advice or a promise of profitability.
